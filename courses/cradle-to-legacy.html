Cradle to Legacy: Build an Enterprise That Can Continue

Move from a tested offer to a practical operating, financial and continuity plan.

By Brian Kennedy Jr., M.Ed.

Get the written course on Gumroad

Your working format

Written lessons, fictional worked examples, practice templates, checks with explanations, and a final project.

Estimated working time: 220 minutes, including exercises. Work at your own pace.

Course-specific videos are not included in this edition. The shared Brian avatar welcome is available below.

Who this is for

  • Early-stage entrepreneurs
  • Students continuing an enterprise experiment
  • Owners reviewing their operations

What you will build

Assemble a purpose/stage map, tested delivery checklist, four-week cash/capacity scenarios, bounded growth experiment and continuity map. Give a five-minute explanation of one growth choice and one risk you will address before expanding.

No paid software, public posting, real outreach or sale is required. Use a private real situation or a clearly labeled fictional case.

How you will know you learned

Complete an observable piece of work, check your reasoning, and revise it using an original instructional rubric. Return to a new case after 14 days.

This is educational practice. No accredited certification or admissions, employment, income or wealth result is promised.

The learning sequence

  1. Connect the enterprise to the life you want to build

    Build: Enterprise purpose and stage map

  2. Make delivery repeatable before adding volume

    Build: Tested delivery checklist

  3. Read cash, costs, and capacity together

    Build: Four-week cash and capacity plan

  4. Choose growth experiments that protect the work

    Build: Bounded growth experiment

  5. Plan continuity, ownership questions, and legacy

    Build: Continuity and legacy question map

A free practice sample

Start with a decision you can name.

Try these first three prompts from “Connect the enterprise to the life you want to build.” Use a notebook or your preferred accessible format. Your answers stay with you; this page collects no worksheet responses.

  1. Purpose / affected people
  2. Current stage / evidence
  3. Income aspiration labeled as goal
Check your reasoning

Does a revenue goal prove that your current offer can support your household?

No. Demand, costs, timing and capacity need evidence.
