Guiding People Through Change: Practical Facilitation

Practice listening, clarify a change, agree on manageable next steps, and teach a bounded session with appropriate referral boundaries.

By Brian Kennedy Jr., M.Ed.

Your working format

Written lessons, fictional worked examples, practice templates, checks with explanations, and a final project.

Estimated working time: 255 minutes, including exercises. Work at your own pace.

Course-specific videos are not included in this edition. The shared Brian avatar welcome is available below.

Who this is for

  • People leaders
  • Educators
  • Community facilitators

What you will build

Submit a change facts/impact map, listening practice, transition record, seven-day agreement, verified-or-labeled support map and a revised 20-minute teach-back. Use fictional cases and no compelled personal disclosure. Explain the limits of your authority and one setting that requires additional expertise.

No paid software, public posting, real outreach or sale is required. Use a private real situation or a clearly labeled fictional case.

How you will know you learned

Complete an observable piece of work, check your reasoning, and revise it using an original instructional rubric. Return to a new case after 14 days.

This is educational practice. No accredited certification or admissions, employment, income or wealth result is promised.

The learning sequence

  1. Describe the change and the people affected

    Build: Change facts and impact map

  2. Listen without interpreting for the other person

    Build: Listening practice and observer notes

  3. Acknowledge what is ending and preserve useful knowledge

    Build: Transition and knowledge-preservation record

  4. Agree on one next step within real authority

    Build: Seven-day action agreement

  5. Keep role boundaries and use the appropriate support route

    Build: Role-boundary and support-route map

  6. Teach back a practical session and revise it

    Build: Reviewed teach-back and revised session kit

A free practice sample

Start with a decision you can name.

Try these first three prompts from “Describe the change and the people affected.” Use a notebook or your preferred accessible format. Your answers stay with you; this page collects no worksheet responses.

  1. Change / source / date
  2. Decided facts / open questions
  3. Affected groups / different practical effects
Check your reasoning

Can a facilitator promise no job or workload effects simply to reassure people?

No. State only what is authorized and verified.
