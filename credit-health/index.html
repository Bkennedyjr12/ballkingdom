A 2-minute self-assessment that gives you a credit health band and a personalized 30/60/90-day roadmap. Everything runs in your browser.

Private by design: nothing you enter is stored or sent anywhere. We never ask for your Social Security number, account numbers, or scores. Refreshing the page clears everything.
Educational only. This is general education, not financial, credit, or legal advice. No score change, loan approval, amount, or rate is promised. Under the law, only information that is inaccurate, incomplete, or unverifiable can be disputed; accurate, current information can't be removed by you or anyone else.
