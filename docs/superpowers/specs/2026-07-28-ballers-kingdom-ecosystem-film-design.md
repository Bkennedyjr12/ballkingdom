# Ballers Kingdom Ecosystem Film — Production Design

## Objective

Create a 70-second, 16:9 unlisted-review master that evolves The Ballers
Kingdom from a narrow soccer montage into a credible ecosystem brand film. The
story begins as a founder manifesto and earns the broader promise through
verified product proof.

## Approved Direction

- Narrative shape: founder manifesto first, product proof second.
- Runtime: 70 seconds.
- CTA: “Choose your path at ballkingdom.com.”
- Voice: Brian’s already authorized professional cloned voice, review-only.
- Delivery: 1920×1080, 24 fps, H.264/AAC, unlisted YouTube review only.

## Audience and Promise

The film speaks to athletes, families, builders, and partners without implying
that every visitor needs the same service. Its unifying promise is a standard
of disciplined development: training remains the emotional foundation, while
the wider Ballers Kingdom surface is presented as distinct, real paths.

## Narrative and Timing

| Time | Movement | Visual proof | Voice / on-screen copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 00–16 | The standard | Brian-led founder portrait and soccer training detail | “The standard does not start at game time. It starts when no one is watching.” / `THE STANDARD` |
| 16–34 | More than the field | approved training and whole-person development imagery | training, academic/career support, consulting, and inner-game language only when verified |

## Visual Language

- Premium editorial documentary rather than a generic sports montage.
- Brian is a calm, authoritative visual anchor; no lip-sync or synthetic
  talking-head motion is required.
- Soccer footage provides physical stakes. Product proof is captured from the
  actual Ballers Kingdom surface, never from invented UI.
- Typography is restrained: one chapter phrase or caption at a time, never a
  large transcript competing with the visual.
- Palette: deep navy, field green, white, and a restrained gold accent.
- All readable text and URLs are composited in post.

## Truth and Asset Rules

1. Independently verify each product, service, URL, and availability statement
   against the current public Ballers Kingdom surface before scripting it.
2. Do not reuse Manus-generated footage, narration, script, captions, avatar,
   or product claims. The supplied archive is a critique benchmark only.
3. Do not present a preview, offline application, booking workflow, partner,
   inspection service, credential, or client outcome as live unless current
   verification supports that statement.
4. Every third-party clip must be licensed and visibly soccer-only; reject
   brand marks, American football, malformed anatomy, fake text, and misleading
   context.
5. The cloned voice stays in the local/unlisted review lane until Brian approves
   a public release.

## Audio and Accessibility

- Final script is the single source of truth for voiceover and captions.
- Produce captions from the final narration; no paraphrased display text.
- Mix voice clearly above a restrained premium instrumental/field-air bed.
- Target broadcast-style intelligibility and maintain an intentional audible
  CTA tail rather than dead air.

## QA and Publication Gates

- Frame-audit every generated or licensed visual source before assembly.
- Review the full master at scene boundaries plus final CTA.
- Verify H.264/AAC, 1920×1080, 24 fps, 70 seconds ±1 second, seekability,
  captions, narration audibility, and final-ten-second audio.
- Upload only after local QA; verify Ballers Kingdom channel, unlisted privacy,
  title, description, thumbnail, processing, and receipt.
- Do not delete or alter current YouTube videos without a separately confirmed
  replacement and explicit deletion authorization.
