# The Ballers Kingdom Flagship Video — Design

**Approved:** 2026-07-25  
**Publishing target:** The Ballers Kingdom YouTube channel (`lilpelejr12@gmail.com`)  
**Format:** public, landscape 16:9, 42 seconds

## Purpose

Establish the channel's contemporary identity with a cinematic brand film that makes clear that The Ballers Kingdom develops the whole athlete: training, mentorship, personal growth, and opportunity beyond sport.

## Audience and action

Primary audience: young athletes and their families in the Inland Empire.  
Secondary audience: coaches, schools, and community partners.

The film sends viewers to `ballkingdom.com` with the closing message: **Build Your Kingdom.**

## Creative direction

This is a grounded, lived-in sports-development film — not a generic motivational montage or a talking-head slide deck. Its visual grammar moves from early field preparation to focused coaching, then broadens into confidence, community, and leadership.

Approved title: **Building Ballers. Advancing Kingdoms.**

## Story and script

| Time | Visual beat | Narration / composited copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–8s | Sunrise field; an athlete prepares and takes a deliberate first touch. | “Every kingdom starts with one decision: show up.” |
| 8–16s | Brian-style coach works one-on-one with an athlete; a correction lands and the athlete tries again. | “Train the skill. Build the discipline.” |
| 16–27s | Homework, a team huddle, and a supportive conversation show development beyond the field. | “Because the game can open doors — but character is what carries you through them.” |
| 27–35s | Athlete leads a drill and reconnects with teammates and family/community. | “We develop leaders ready for the next play and the next chapter.” |
| 35–42s | Calm, confident close over a real field environment and branded end card. | “The Ballers Kingdom. Build your kingdom.” |

All readable text, logo treatment, and the website will be composited in post. The video model must not generate text.

## Continuity and brand rules

- Use only licensed, supplied, or generated-on-purpose visuals; no unlicensed team footage or music.
- Do not create a false likeness of Brian or use a cloned voice without a separate authorization and QA pass.
- Keep scenes inclusive, energetic, and credible: natural athletic effort, real coach-athlete exchange, and visible community.
- Reuse site-provided Ballers Kingdom branding and website language. No unsupported outcome, scholarship, recruiting, or professional-sports claims.

## Render and publishing plan

1. Build the `video-engine` production package: brief, character/continuity notes, beat map, shot prompts, audio plan, render plan, and QA checklist.
2. Create a low-cost review master with intentionally designed visual scenes, post-composited graphics, licensed/owned audio, and original narration or text-led audio.
3. Inspect each scene and complete technical/perceptual QA: H.264/AAC, 1280×720 or higher, 24 fps, audio presence, clean last 10 seconds, seekability, spelling, and legibility.
4. Upload the passing master publicly to the authorized Ballers Kingdom YouTube channel with a descriptive title, channel-appropriate description, thumbnail, and the `ballkingdom.com` destination.
5. Preserve the local package and the upload receipt; do not commit rendered media or credentials.

## Acceptance criteria

- The story clearly communicates athlete development beyond sport.
- The result feels cinematic and specific to Ballers Kingdom, not templated.
- All claims and on-screen copy are verified against the site.
- The final visual and audio hold through the CTA; no dead-air tail.
- The published video appears on the Ballers Kingdom YouTube channel as Public, with processing completed.
