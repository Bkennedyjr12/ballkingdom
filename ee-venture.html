The Framework · Five Tracks

One holistic consulting model.

The Ballers Kingdom Consulting model isn't a single curriculum. It's an integrated framework that meets each person where they are — and develops the whole person across five connected tracks. We start with the track you need most, then build outward.

L

Life

Personal coaching. Mindset, identity, faith, family. The foundation under everything else.

A

Academic

Study habits, executive function, college prep — built on Brian's M.Ed. in Guidance & Counseling.

C

Career

Vocational planning, resume, networks, interview prep — for first jobs through career pivots.

B

Business

Entrepreneurial plans, financial literacy, brand-building, and SBA-readiness for early founders.

D

Digital

Modern toolkit — websites, social, automation, and AI for personal brand and small-business operations.

A holistic model for building beyond the game.

The Youth Venture Program was the seed. The Ballers Kingdom Consulting model is the practice it grew into — a holistic consulting and coaching engagement for youth and adults who want development that goes wider than soccer.

Brian's professional practice spans civic service, entrepreneurial development, and educational administration. The Consulting framework brings that full range to the table — whether you're 14 and writing your first business plan, or 34 and pivoting careers. Every engagement is tailored, but it runs on the same five-track model.
