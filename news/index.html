October 15, 2026 · CFPB

CFPB updates its Consumer Credit Trends dashboards

The CFPB refreshed its monthly dashboards tracking originations and inquiries for mortgages, credit cards, auto loans, and student loans — a useful pulse on how consumers are faring in credit markets.

October 12, 2026 · Consumer Financial Services Law Monitor

CFPB's 2025 Fair Lending Report signals a narrower enforcement focus

Released October 1, the Bureau's 2025 Fair Lending Report describes a shift toward cases of intentional discrimination with identifiable victims, alongside changes to Regulation B and the §1071 small-business data rule.

October 9, 2026 · American Banker

SBA starts fiscal 2027 with a tighter credit box

SBA 7(a) approvals fell to about $32.4 billion across 56,130 loans in fiscal 2026, with 504 volume down 9%, as stricter underwriting rules took hold. A second rules revision took effect October 1.

September 22, 2026 · Forbes

SBA loan changes on Oct. 1 mean more paperwork for many borrowers

Revised SBA rules bring dozens of updates to loan processing — especially for business acquisitions — with more documentation and potentially longer waits for some smaller deals.

SBA issues SOP 50 10 8.1 for 7(a) and 504 loans

The SBA published the updated Standard Operating Procedure governing its 7(a) and 504 lender programs, effective October 1, 2026. Owners planning an SBA application should ask lenders how it affects their request.
