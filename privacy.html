Privacy Policy

Effective August 21, 2026

The Ballers Kingdom (“we,” “us,” or “our”) provides training, consulting, inspection-related education, booking, and invoicing services. This policy explains how we handle information collected through ballkingdom.com and related service workflows.

Information we collect

  • Contact and booking information, such as name, email address, phone number, selected service, appointment details, and messages you submit.
  • Business and transaction information needed to prepare estimates, invoices, and service records.
  • Basic website and device information used for security, reliability, and performance.

How we use information

We use information to respond to requests, schedule and deliver services, send appointment confirmations, prepare and deliver manually approved invoices, maintain accounting records, prevent misuse, and comply with legal obligations.

Service providers

We use service providers only as needed to operate our business. These may include Microsoft 365 for email, Intuit QuickBooks Online for accounting and invoices, Firebase/Google Cloud for application hosting and secure processing, and payment providers selected at checkout. Their handling of information is governed by their own policies and our configurations.

QuickBooks data

When our internal integration is connected to QuickBooks Online, it accesses accounting data only to create and retrieve customer invoices and related records authorized by The Ballers Kingdom. QuickBooks credentials are stored securely and are not exposed to website visitors.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers, professional advisers, or authorities when reasonably necessary to operate services, protect rights and security, or meet legal requirements.

Retention and security

We retain information only as long as reasonably needed for service, accounting, security, and legal purposes. We use access controls, encrypted provider connections, and restricted credential storage, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of information, subject to legal and accounting retention requirements. You may also ask us to disconnect our QuickBooks integration.

Contact

Email info@ballkingdom.com with privacy questions or requests.
