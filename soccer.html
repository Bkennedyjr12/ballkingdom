Philosophy

We don't train you harder. We train you smarter.

Three questions every session: can you read the game, can you execute under pressure, and can you do it when you're tired? That's how championships are won.

T

Technical

First touch, weight of pass, finishing in the 18, 1v1 attacking and defending.

Tactical

Positional awareness, reading transition, decision-making under fatigue.

M

Mental

Competitive mindset, handling setbacks, college-level preparation.
