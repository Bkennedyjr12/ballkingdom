# Provider Scene Prompts

All prompts require photoreal 16:9 base footage with no text, logos, badges, numbers, UI, watermark, or lettering. Use only the original continuity-lock characters.

## Scene 1 — First touch (8s)

Sunrise community soccer field, original youth athlete in the locked navy-and-cream kit receives a grounded pass, cushions a deliberate first touch, then dribbles into open grass. Low tracking camera follows the ball to their focused face; teammates stretch and pass naturally in the distant background. Dew, breath, soft field ambience, authentic athletic movement. No readable text.

## Scene 2 — Coach correction (8s)

Same field and original characters. The original coach demonstrates an open body position beside the athlete, the athlete watches closely, retries the movement, and the coach gives a restrained nod. Medium moving two-shot; teammates pause, watch, then resume their drill. Natural human reactions, purposeful camera move, no readable text.

## Scene 3 — Sport-plus-life development (11s)

Same athlete closes a blank homework notebook at a sideline table, joins a tight team huddle, then exchanges a warm, quiet glance with a supporting adult at the field edge. A teammate taps the athlete's shoulder to bring them into the huddle. Handheld-to-stable camera transition motivated by the huddle closing. No generated writing anywhere.

## Scene 4 — Athlete leadership (8s)

Same athlete places cones, demonstrates a simple passing pattern, and waves teammates into the next repetition. Teammates make eye contact, respond, and move through the drill; coach observes from the edge with an approving but understated reaction. Wide steadicam orbit, living field background, no readable text.

## Scene 5 — Lasting CTA (7s)

Same community field. The full group plays through the drill while the original athlete looks toward the next pass; camera slowly pulls back into warm morning light. Hold the final composition for post-composited verified Ballers Kingdom graphics while training and ambience continue. No generated text or logos.
