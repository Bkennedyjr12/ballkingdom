**Goal:** Show Inland Empire young athletes and families that The Ballers Kingdom develops skill, discipline, character, and opportunity beyond sport.

**Format:** 42 seconds, YouTube, 16:9, photoreal directed scenes. Grounded sunrise-field training, genuine coach-athlete exchange, family/community warmth, and premium navy/cream/green graphics composited only in post.

**Guardrails:** Use only licensed, supplied, or purpose-generated visuals and original/licensed music. Do not generate readable text, logos, URLs, metrics, or claims. Do not emulate Brian or any real person; the coach is an original, non-celebrity adult character. No cloned voice.
