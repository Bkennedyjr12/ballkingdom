# Character Bible

## Brian — adult coach continuity reference

Black man in his 30s with short natural hair and a neatly trimmed beard. He wears the same plain black training jacket and black athletic pants in every scene; the clothing carries no readable mark. His performance is calm and precise: he demonstrates, observes, waits for the athlete's response, then gives a restrained nod. His likeness reference is user-authorized for adult-coach continuity only.

## Original teenage soccer athlete

Male, age 15–17, plain dark unmarked training kit, focused expression, and age-appropriate build. He receives correction, makes a second attempt, solves the pressure drill, and returns to play with visible confidence. He is an original character, not a reference likeness.

## Original teammate

Comparable teenage age, plain unmarked kit, supportive but competitive. The teammate reacts to the first touch and pressure decision, then continues the drill naturally.

## Original guardian

Adult in casual sideline clothing, attentive rather than staged. The guardian receives a concise coaching update and watches the athlete re-enter play.
