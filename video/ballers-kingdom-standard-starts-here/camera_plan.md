# Camera and Light Plan

Use a grounded 24–50mm field language: low gimbal movement for arrival, controlled handheld for correction and pressure, then a smoother gimbal observation for connection and invitation. Movement is motivated by the ball, a correction, a pressure release, or the athlete's return to play. Use warm late-afternoon side light, soft field bounce, and natural practical light only. Protect clean frame space in scene five for post graphics.
