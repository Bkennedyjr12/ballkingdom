# Creative Brief

**Goal:** Show a real coaching relationship where skill, discipline, confidence, and family support develop the whole athlete.

**Audience:** Young soccer athletes, families, coaches, schools, and community partners in the Inland Empire.

**Format:** 45 seconds, YouTube landscape 16:9, planned 1280×720 at 24fps.

**Visual grammar:** A warm late-afternoon Southern California community field, grounded physical action, handheld detail work, and measured gimbal movement. Every scene contains a coach/athlete exchange, a response, motivated camera movement, and active background life. Generated footage is text-free; verified title, captions, logo treatment, website, and CTA are composited only in post.

**Tone:** Disciplined, warm, specific, grounded, and confident. This is a lived coaching moment, not a promise of outcomes.
