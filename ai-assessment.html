How the snapshot works

Each answer adds 0, 1, 2, or 3 points. Four questions contribute to each area, for a maximum of 12 per area and 36 overall. All twelve answers are required.

Totals of 0–11 suggest building the basics, 12–23 suggest testing a focused pilot, and 24–36 suggest improving a working practice. The lowest-scoring areas are highlighted; tied areas receive equal attention.

These ranges are planning prompts. They are not a validated benchmark, certification, or prediction. Results use the same fixed calculation for everyone; no AI model generates the report.
